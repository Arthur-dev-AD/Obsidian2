# AXE I

## A few facts about Hadrian's wall

The wall is located in the north of England near the Scottish border. It extend from the Irish sea in the west to the North sea in the east. It started construction in 120 AD (avant J.C.) and took 15 000 mens 6 years to complete. It was made to deflect attacks from the native Britons, to control inter-territories trades and immigration. Along it's full length, there is 89 castles , 157 turrets and 15 large forts. The live there was far from ostere. It was even kind of comfy, they had public baths, something romans loved overal, there was also underfloor heating in officers barracks, flushing latrines and drainage for sewage and storm water.
